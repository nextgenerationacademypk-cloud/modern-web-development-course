# Day 12 — Selectors, colours, and typography

**Week:** 3 · **Session:** 12 of 40 · **Duration:** 120 minutes

## Purpose
Teach element, class, and ID selectors. Practise colour, background-color, font-family, font-size, font-weight, line-height, and text-align. Use a small consistent colour palette.

## Prerequisite
Students have completed Week 2 HTML lessons and can preview an HTML page.

## Learning outcome
Choose a matching selector and style headings, paragraphs, links, and a highlighted notice.
