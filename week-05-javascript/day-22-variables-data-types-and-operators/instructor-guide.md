# Day 22 Instructor Guide — Variables, Data Types and Operators

## Teaching purpose
Students move from printing fixed messages to storing and calculating with values. Teach each concept with small console examples.

## Prepare
- Open today's HTML and JavaScript example.
- Prepare a simple price-and-quantity calculation on the board.
- Review strings, numbers, booleans, `const`, and `let`.

## Outcomes
Students declare variables, identify three basic types, perform arithmetic, and compare values.

## 120-minute plan
20 min explanation · 4 min theory review · 30 min demo · 62 min practice · 4 min review.

## Demonstration sequence
1. Declare a string with `const`.
2. Declare a number with `let` and reassign it.
3. Declare a boolean.
4. Show `typeof` for each value.
5. Calculate a total using multiplication.
6. Demonstrate addition, subtraction, division, and remainder.
7. Compare two numbers with `===` and `>`.
8. Change values and predict the output before refreshing.

## Teaching cautions
- Keep the focus on primitive string, number, and boolean values.
- Do not introduce objects, arrays, or type coercion in detail.
- Explain that `const` prevents reassignment, not that every object or collection is deeply immutable.
- Emphasise `===` for comparisons; avoid teaching loose equality now.

## Common mistakes
- Quotes around numbers.
- Incorrect capitalisation in names.
- Reassigning a `const`.
- Confusing `=` and `===`.
- Expecting `7 / 2` to produce a whole number.

## Support and extension
**Support:** Give students a partly completed example with named price, quantity, and total variables.
**Extension:** Ask students to calculate a discount and compare the total with a fixed budget.

## Checkpoint
Student explains one variable, identifies its type, and predicts the result of one arithmetic and one comparison expression.
