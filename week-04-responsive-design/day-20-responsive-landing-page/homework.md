# Day 20 Homework — Finish Your Responsive Landing Page

## Task
Complete your one-page landing page from class.

## Required sections
- Header and navigation.
- Hero section.
- At least three feature/course cards.
- Footer.

## Technical requirements
- Viewport meta tag.
- Mobile-first CSS.
- At least one media query.
- Flexbox or Grid.
- Responsive images if images are used.
- No unintended horizontal scrolling at 375px, 768px, and 1280px.

## Submit
- HTML file.
- CSS file.
- Three screenshots or a short written test report covering the three widths.
- A short explanation of one responsive issue you found and fixed.

## Optional challenge
Add a second breakpoint or improve the navigation spacing on small screens.

## Safety
Use fictional content. Do not publish private phone numbers, home addresses, personal email addresses, or passwords.
