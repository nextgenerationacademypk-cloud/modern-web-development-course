# Day 29 Homework — Friendly Form

## Task
Create a simple form for adding a fictional course enquiry or practice task.

## Requirements
- A connected label and text input.
- A submit button.
- A feedback paragraph.
- A submit event listener.
- preventDefault() inside the listener.
- trim() and an empty-value check.
- A helpful success message for valid input.

## Submit
Provide a screenshot of the form and the JavaScript code. Write one sentence explaining why server-side validation is needed in real applications.

## Marking — 5 marks
- Correct form and label: 1
- Submit event and preventDefault: 1
- Reads and trims input: 1
- Correct feedback for invalid and valid values: 1
- Explanation and safe sample content: 1

Do not collect or publish real personal information.
