## Readiness guidance

- **Ready:** The student can complete the core file, browser, VS Code, and preview tasks independently or with only small reminders.
- **Ready with support:** The student can begin the main course but needs short guided practice. Agree on the support steps below.
- **Needs catch-up:** Several essential tasks are not yet completed. Arrange a short practice session and check again. Do not shame or automatically exclude the student.
